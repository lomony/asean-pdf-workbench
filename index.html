ASEAN Secretariat · PDF Workbench (unofficial)

View, search and reorganise PDFs in your browser

Read, search, rotate, delete, insert and reorder pages, merge and split documents, and open password-protected files. No installation and no account needed.

Open PDF Workbench

The first load downloads up to about 40 MB, so it can take a moment. Works best in a current Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer.

Files stay on your device

Everything runs locally in your browser. Documents are not uploaded to any server.

Early alpha software

Based on PDFCraft 0.6.0. Always keep the original file and check the result before sharing.

Digital signing not verified

The app includes E-Sign and certificate tools, but they have not been tested for ASEAN Secretariat use. Sign official documents with your approved software.

Unofficial tool

Not an official ASEAN Secretariat service. Check with IT before using it for official or confidential documents.
